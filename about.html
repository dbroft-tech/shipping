About RGR Logistics

Your trusted partner in global logistics solutions

Who We Are

RGR Logistics Ltd is a leading logistics company specializing in comprehensive supply chain solutions. With our tagline "Logistics Expert," we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for businesses seeking reliable clearing and forwarding, import/export, transportation, and sourcing services.

Based in Nsambya Katwe, Ring Road, we serve clients across multiple international markets including China, Dubai, India, and Turkey. Our extensive network and deep industry expertise enable us to provide seamless logistics solutions that drive business growth.

Our Mission

To provide world-class logistics services that enable our clients to focus on their core business while we handle the complexities of global trade and transportation.

Our Vision
