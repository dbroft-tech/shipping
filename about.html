About RGR Logistics

Your trusted partner in bonded warehouse and clearing services

Who We Are

RGR Logistics Ltd is a private institution registered and incorporated in Uganda as a company limited by shares (Reg. NO: 80034281964094). We were incorporated in 2025 to offer a professional response to bonded warehouses and the needs of the growing bonded warehouse industry in Uganda.

RGR Logistics Ltd is a client-centric company involved in Warehouse, depot, and clearing services. Our head office is located at KEVINA, KAMPALA, MAKINDYE DIVISION WEST, MAKINDYE DIVISION, KATWE II, KEVINA - strategically positioned for quick customs clearances of both import and export transportation.

The company is recognized for its excellent services and performance in the bonded warehouse value chain by the Uganda Revenue Authority.

Our Mission

To provide professional and reliable bonded warehouse, depot, and clearing services that meet the growing needs of Uganda's logistics industry while maintaining the highest standards recognized by regulatory authorities.

Our Vision

To be the leading bonded warehouse and clearing service provider in Uganda, recognized for excellence and professional response to industry needs.
