Rgr Logistics Ltd is a private institution registered and incorporated in Uganda as a company limited by shares (Reg. NO: 80034281964094). It was incorporated in 2025 to offer a professional response to bonded warehouses and the needs of the growing bonded warehouse industry in Uganda.
Rgr Logistics Ltd is a client-centric company involved in Warehouse, depot, clearing. The company head office is located at KEVINA, KAMPALA, MAKINDYE DIVISION WEST, MAKINDYE DIVISION,KATWE II,KEVINA appropriate for quick customs clearances of both import and export transportation. The company is recognized for its excellent services and performance in the bonded warehouse value chain by the Uganda Revenue Authority.
