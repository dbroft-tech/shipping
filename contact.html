Frequently Asked Questions

How long does customs clearance take?

Customs clearance typically takes 1-3 business days, depending on the complexity of the shipment and documentation completeness.

Do you provide door-to-door delivery?

Yes, we offer comprehensive door-to-door logistics solutions including pickup, transportation, customs clearance, and final delivery.

What documents do I need for international shipping?

Required documents typically include commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, and any specific permits or certificates for your cargo type.

Can you handle oversized or special cargo?

Yes, we have experience handling oversized, heavy, and special cargo including machinery, vehicles, and project cargo with specialized equipment.
